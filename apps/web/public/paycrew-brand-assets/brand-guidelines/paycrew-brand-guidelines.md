# usePayCrew Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea
The forward-moving arrow is the core symbol: speed, money movement, progress, and connection. Keep it flat, geometric, and immediately legible. The wordmark spelling is **usePayCrew**.

## Core palette
| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Move Green | `#39FF14` | Primary accent, action, icon |
| Deep Green | `#0B3D2E` | Main text, dark surfaces, trust |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed logo and clean surfaces |
| Black | `#000000` | Monochrome printing |
| Soft Neutral | `#F4F7F5` | UI background |
| Pale Green | `#E9F8E8` | Subtle surfaces |

## Typography
- Recommended: **Inter** for product UI and marketing; **Manrope** as an alternative for a slightly more expressive headline.
- Wordmark source currently uses editable SVG text with Arial/Helvetica fallback. For a locked production master, convert the wordmark text to outlines in a vector editor after selecting the approved font.
- Use sentence case for product copy. Prefer concise, clear language.

## Clear space
Maintain clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the icon's inner arrow opening (roughly 1/4 of icon height). No text, edges, or other marks should enter this area.

## Minimum sizes
- Full horizontal logo: 140 px wide digital minimum; 32 mm print minimum.
- Logo with tagline: 280 px wide digital minimum; remove tagline below this width.
- Standalone icon: 16 px minimum for favicon; use the simplified favicon/app icon artwork at tiny sizes.
- Dashboard sidebar: icon-only below 120 px available width.

## Recommended use
- Website / pitch deck: primary horizontal logo.
- Website footer / document cover: horizontal logo with tagline where space permits.
- Mobile app / social profile: rounded-square app icon.
- Favicon: supplied 16, 32, and 48 px assets.
- Dashboard: compact icon-only or horizontal logo depending on sidebar width.
- Email signature: compact horizontal SVG.
- Invoices and formal documents: black or deep-green monochrome logo.
- Dark surfaces: white wordmark with Move Green icon, or all-white monochrome.

## Correct use
- Keep original proportions; scale uniformly.
- Use the supplied light/dark variants on their intended backgrounds.
- Keep the icon flat and high contrast.
- Use Move Green as an accent rather than filling every interface element with it.

## Avoid
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, or redraw the arrow.
- Do not add bevels, 3D effects, glow, or heavy shadows.
- Do not place the green icon on a similarly bright background without a contrasting container.
- Do not recolor the wordmark arbitrarily or add a dollar/coin/crypto symbol.
- Do not use the tagline at tiny sizes.

## Export notes
- SVGs are scalable. PNGs are generated from the same SVG source.
- Transparent PNG exports are placed in `png/` and individual asset folders. Files with a deliberately colored background (app/social/OG assets) are not intended to be transparent.
- Favicon and app icon files are square, with exact dimensions in their filenames.
